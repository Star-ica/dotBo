WHAT YOU GET
🔗
Universal Resolver

Map your .bot name to a wallet address, smart contract, IPFS hash, or any onchain entity. One name, infinite resolution.

📋
Rich Onchain Records

Store your Twitter, GitHub, email, bio, and custom key-value pairs directly onchain — your verifiable profile.

🌿
Subname Issuance

Own myproject.bot? Issue team.myproject.bot to collaborators. Build hierarchical identities for your protocol or community.

⛓️
Native to BOT Chain

Zero gas surprises. Contracts live on BOT Chain with fast finality. No bridge, no overhead.

🔐
Full Ownership

Your name is an NFT. Transfer, sell, or delegate — the registry enforces your ownership without a middleman.

🕐
Renewable Leases

Names are leased annually. Renew anytime before expiry. Expired names re-enter the open market after a grace period.
